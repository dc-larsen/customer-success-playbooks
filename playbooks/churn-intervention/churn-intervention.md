# Churn Intervention Playbook

## 🎯 Purpose
A repeatable play for when an account is at risk. Diagnose the real reason fast, act before the renewal, and know when a save is no longer worth the effort.

## 🚨 When to Run This
Trigger the play the moment an account goes **Red** in [Renewal Risk Governance](../renewal-risk-governance/renewal-risk-governance.md), or when one of these shows up:
- **Usage drops** sharply (seats, value activity, or logins)
- **The champion leaves** or goes quiet
- **A key integration** is removed
- **Support friction** stacks up (escalations, slow resolutions, a painful bug)
- **A budget or economic signal** lands (hiring freeze, reorg, "we're reviewing our tools")

## 🔍 Step 1: Diagnose the Real Reason
The stated reason is rarely the real one. "Too expensive" often means "we never proved value."

- Map the symptom to a cause: low adoption, lost champion, unmet original goal, competitor, product gap, or budget
- Pull the [Health Scoring](../health-scoring/health-scoring.md) trends to separate a real drop from noise
- Write the cause down in one sentence. If you can't, you don't understand it yet.

## 🗣️ Step 2: Confirm With the Customer
- Have the direct conversation. Don't diagnose a save from your dashboard alone.
- Ask what changed and what would have to be true for them to stay
- Listen for the economic buyer's view, not only the daily user's

## 📝 Step 3: Build the Save Plan
- Tie it back to the **original problem** you captured during [Onboarding](../onboarding/onboarding.md). That problem is your strongest argument.
- Pick one **quick win** you can land in two weeks to rebuild momentum
- Set a clear success measure and a date

## 🧵 Step 4: Multi-Thread
- A one-champion account is a single point of failure. Build a second and third relationship.
- Reach the **economic buyer** before the renewal window, not inside it
- Map who benefits if you stay and who signs the renewal. They are not always the same person.

## 📈 Step 5: Re-Establish Value
- Re-onboard the feature tied to their core problem
- Run an executive business review: remind leadership what they bought and what it has returned
- Put the proof in their language: their KPI, their dollars, their risk reduced

## 🆘 Step 6: Escalate Internally
- Bring in your exec sponsor for an exec-to-exec conversation
- Ask product for a specific commitment if a gap is the blocker, with a date you can share
- Loop in pricing or packaging help early if cost is the real issue

## 🛑 Step 7: Know When It's Lost
- Not every account is savable, and over-investing in a lost one costs you two healthy ones
- When the problem is gone (the team, the budget, the use case), run a clean offboarding
- Leave the door open. A respectful exit is the start of the win-back.

## ⏱️ Run It Against the Clock
- **90+ days out:** diagnose, confirm, and start the quick win
- **30-60 days:** prove value, multi-thread, escalate if it's still Red
- **Inside 30 days:** secure the economic buyer and the paperwork. Stop opening new workstreams.

- **[Renewal Risk Governance](../renewal-risk-governance/renewal-risk-governance.md)** → every Red triggers this play
- **[Health Scoring](../health-scoring/health-scoring.md)** → the trend data behind the diagnosis
- **[Proactive Alerts](../alerts/alerts.md)** → the early-warning signals that fire the play
- **[Onboarding](../onboarding/onboarding.md)** → the original problem you defend

---

*You can't save an account you diagnosed wrong. Find the real reason, prove value against it, and know when to walk.*
