# Call Review Automation

**Grade your own customer calls every week, from the transcript**

You run dozens of customer calls a month and review almost none of them. Quality drifts and you don't notice. An automated review loop scores every call against a rubric you set, so you get specific feedback without re-listening to anything.

This playbook covers how to build that loop.

---

## 1. Capture the transcripts

Use whatever records your calls: Granola, Gong, Zoom, Fathom. You need the text, not the audio. Most tools export or sync a transcript you can pull automatically.

---

## 2. Define the rubric

Decide what a good call does, then write it as a checklist the assistant can score against. For a CS call, that might be:
- Opened on their problem, not your product
- Confirmed the value they're after
- Surfaced risks or blockers
- Set a clear next step with an owner and a date
- Found a second relationship beyond the main contact

Keep it to the handful of beats that actually predict a good outcome.

---

## 3. Score each call

Feed the transcript and the rubric to the assistant. Ask for a score per beat, one specific example of what worked, and one thing to do better next time. Specific beats vague: "you never set a next step" is useful, "good call" is not.

---

## 4. Make it a weekly habit

Roll the scores into a weekly digest to yourself: average per beat, your weakest beat, one call worth re-reading. Fix one beat at a time.

---

## 5. Handle customer data carefully

Transcripts contain customer information. Keep them in tools you're cleared to use, and don't paste them where they shouldn't go.

---

## 6. Connection to other playbooks

- **Claude Code Skills:** Build the grader as a skill you run on a schedule.
- **Onboarding, Renewals, Expansion:** The calls you're grading, and the beats each should hit.
- **AI Second Brain:** File recurring feedback so the lessons compound.

---

## Final thought

You can't improve calls you never review. Let the transcript do the reviewing, and spend your time fixing the one beat that matters most this week.
